The harvest

Plots marked Failed gave nothing worth picking. Plots marked Not recorded were picked without anyone writing it down, which is not the same as failed. Plot 200 is an example deep in the register; the link opens the table to reach it.

How the register was kept

Each group wrote its plots on the shed whiteboard, and the kaimahi (staff) copied the board into the register every Friday, then weighed a sample of thirty plots to check it.

Decision needed

The north terrace lost most of its beans to wind in December. A windbreak of flax along its edge would take one working bee, and it is what goal 2 asks for.

Words used here

Working bee
A day when everyone comes to do one big job together.
Raised bed
A bed built up above the ground, easier on knees and quicker to warm.
Companion planting
Growing plants side by side because each helps the other.
